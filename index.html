About Our Resort

Nestled along the picturesque Masaka Highway in Mpigi, ZAL PALM RESORT offers an oasis of tranquility and luxury. Our resort combines modern amenities with natural beauty to create an unforgettable experience for our guests.

With over a decade of excellence in hospitality, we pride ourselves on providing exceptional service, exquisite cuisine, and a serene environment for relaxation and rejuvenation.

Our resort features beautifully appointed accommodations, a fine dining restaurant, event spaces for special occasions, and our unique natural fish pond that adds to the tranquil atmosphere.

Our Natural Fish Pond

At the heart of ZAL PALM RESORT lies our serene natural fish pond, a unique feature that sets us apart. This tranquil water feature not only adds to the beauty of our landscape but also provides a relaxing atmosphere for our guests.

Our fish pond is home to various species of fish and is surrounded by lush vegetation, creating a perfect spot for meditation, photography, or simply enjoying the peaceful ambiance.

Guests can enjoy feeding the fish, learning about aquatic ecosystems, or simply relaxing by the water's edge with a refreshing drink from our bar.
